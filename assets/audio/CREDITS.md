# Audio credits

All impact sounds in this folder come from one library:

- **Kenney — Impact Sounds** (130-file pack)
  Source page: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  Licence: **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Attribution is not required under CC0; this record is kept for provenance.
  Files were fetched 2026-11-09 from a verbatim mirror of the pack's files
  (the pack page itself bot-blocks direct downloads from this machine) and
  downmixed to mono with ffmpeg; no other processing.
